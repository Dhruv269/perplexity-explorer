03 · How It Combines

From Token Probabilities to Perplexity

Mathematical Relationship
Perplexity = ( 1/P₁ × 1/P₂ × … × 1/Pₙ )1/N
Run an analysis above to see your sentence's exact probabilities plugged directly into this formula.
Intuition in Plain English
  • 1 Every token gets a probability. As the model reads left to right, it scores how likely the next token was.
  • 2 Invert to measure surprise (1/P). A 50% probability gives a small factor of 2; a 1% probability gives a huge factor of 100.
  • 3 Take the geometric mean (N-th root). Multiplying all factors and taking the N-th root averages out sentence length.
  • 4 Lower means less surprised. A perplexity of 8 means the model felt as uncertain as rolling a fair 8-sided die at each token.

07 · Key Takeaways

What Perplexity Tells Us About Language Models

01 / CONDITIONING

Context Shapes Every Guess

A token isn't inherently probable or improbable in isolation — "Paris" is surprising at the start of a sentence, yet nearly certain after "The capital of France is".

02 / SENSITIVITY

One Anomaly Spikes the Score

Because perplexity multiplies 1/P across tokens, a single unexpected word with P = 0.001 imposes a 1000× penalty that lifts the entire sentence's perplexity.

03 / BRANCHING FACTOR

Effective Uncertainty per Step

Think of perplexity as how many equally likely choices the model effectively had at each token. Lower perplexity means sharper, more confident predictions.
